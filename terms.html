    Terms & Privacy Policy

    Operational transparency and client data protection standards conforming with Kenyan regulatory frameworks.

    1. Vehicle Reservation & Deposit Policy

    To secure an exclusive 48-hour reservation hold on any vehicle in our Nairobi inventory, customers may place a reservation deposit via Safaricom M-Pesa or direct RTGS bank transfer. Upon payment, the vehicle is marked as Reserved and will not be demonstrated or sold to another buyer. If the client declines purchase upon physical inspection, the reservation deposit is refunded in full within 2 business days.

    2. Odometer & Provenance Guarantee

    Kayan & Njogu Motors warrants that all direct imports are cleared under authentic auction grading standards and backed by valid pre-export inspection certificates. Chassis numbers, Japanese deregistration certificates, and KRA Simba entries are provided to the buyer during vehicle handover.

    3. Privacy Policy & Data Security

    In compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, customer personal information (names, national ID numbers, phone numbers, and banking details) is collected strictly for vehicle invoicing, NTSA TIMS logbook transfer, and asset financing processing. We never sell or share client data with third-party advertisers.

    4. Payment Security Notice

    All official transactions must be conducted through our verified company accounts: Kayan & Njogu Motors Ltd (Paybill: 889900) or our official corporate bank accounts. We will never ask customers to send funds to personal employee mobile numbers.
