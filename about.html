    Why Nairobi Discerning Drivers Choose Us

    1. Auction Grade 4.5+ Only

    We never purchase repaired or accident-damaged vehicles. Every car includes the original Japanese or British auction sheet.

    2. Genuine Odometer Guarantee

    Verified through JEVIC and QISJ pre-export inspection certificates before shipping to Kenya.

    3. 48-Hour NTSA Plate Handover

    All customs duty is fully paid in advance through KRA Simba/iCMS so your car is registered in your name within 48 hours.

    4. Tailored Financing & Trade-In

    Direct liaison with major bank credit committees for rapid asset finance approval up to 80%.
