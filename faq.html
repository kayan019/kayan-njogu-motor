    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find transparent answers regarding vehicle import requirements, KRA duty clearance, bank asset financing, and M-Pesa reservations.

    1. Are all vehicles in your showroom verified for authentic mileage? +
    Yes. Every vehicle we source from Japan or the United Kingdom comes with original pre-export inspection certificates (JEVIC, QISJ, or UK MOT service history). We have a strict zero-tolerance policy against odometer rollback.
    2. Is customs duty and registration included in the listed price? +
    Yes. All prices quoted on our website are inclusive of full KRA customs duty, port clearance fees, Railway Development Levy (RDL), Import Declaration Fee (IDF), and initial NTSA registration plate processing. There are zero hidden surprises.
    3. How does the M-Pesa vehicle reservation work? +
    When you identify a vehicle you wish to secure, you can initiate a reservation deposit (KSh 50,000 or KSh 100,000) through our official Safaricom Daraja STK Push prompt. Once authorized, the car is placed on 48-hour exclusive hold in your name and marked Reserved on our showroom floor. If upon physical inspection the vehicle fails to meet your expectations, your reservation deposit is refunded in full.
    4. Which banks do you partner with for asset financing? +
    We work directly with NCBA Bank, Stanbic Bank, Equity Bank, Co-operative Bank, and I&M Bank. Depending on your credit profile and bank statements, asset financing covers up to 80% of the vehicle value with repayment tenures of up to 60 months.
    5. Can I order a specific car not currently in the Nairobi showroom? +
    Absolutely. Our CEO Kayan Njogu personally bids on Japanese auto auctions (USS, TAA, CAA) and procures UK main dealer stock on client demand. Bespoke imports typically arrive at Mombasa port within 35 to 45 days.
    6. Do you accept trade-ins? +
    Yes, we accept clean, well-maintained vehicles for trade-in towards any vehicle in our showroom. You can submit your car details on our Sell Your Car page for an initial estimate.
